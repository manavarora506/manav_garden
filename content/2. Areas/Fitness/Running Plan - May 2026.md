# Running Plan - May 2026

Goal for the rest of May: ease into running without disrupting strength training. Target **1–2 runs per week** and finish the month comfortable with **20–30 minutes** of easy walk/run or jogging.

## Principles

- Keep every run **easy** — conversational pace.
- Use walk/run intervals; no pace chasing this month.
- Avoid running the day after heavy legs if possible.
- Stop if there is sharp shin, knee, foot, or Achilles pain.
- Success metric: **show up, finish easy, and stay healthy.**

## Weekly placement

Best days:
- After upper-body days
- On lighter workout days
- After a rest day

Avoid:
- Day after heavy squats/deadlifts/leg volume
- Stacking hard basketball/tennis + running + legs too close together

Example rhythm:
- Monday: Lift
- Tuesday: Easy run
- Wednesday: Lift
- Thursday: Lift or sport
- Friday: Easy run or incline walk
- Saturday: Basketball/tennis/lift
- Sunday: Rest/walk

## Form cues

- Run slower than you think.
- Shorter, quicker steps.
- Land quietly.
- Stay relaxed through shoulders and hands.
- Flat routes or treadmill are best for now.
