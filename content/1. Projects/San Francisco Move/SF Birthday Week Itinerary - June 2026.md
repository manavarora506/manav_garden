# SF Birthday Week Itinerary - June 2026

Assumption: birthday is Thursday, June 4, 2026; Monday is June 1. Calendar search found a Jun 3 "Walk with Seana" event, so this plan assumes the birthday week is for Seana.

## Vibe

First SF birthday, 26th birthday, nature + creatures + artsy/creative + film. Make it feel like a curated "first birthday in San Francisco" week rather than one oversized day.

## Monday, June 1 — Soft launch: Hayes / Alamo / film-night tone-setter

- Afternoon/evening walk: Hayes Valley → Alamo Square golden hour.
- Small surprise: flowers + card introducing the week: "26 in SF: a tiny field guide."
- Dinner: low-pressure neighborhood dinner in Hayes/Divis/NoPa.
- Optional film: Roxie, Alamo Drafthouse New Mission, AMC Kabuki, or Apple Cinemas Van Ness depending on showtimes.

## Tuesday, June 2 — Creatures + wonder day

Primary option:
- California Academy of Sciences: aquarium, rainforest dome, planetarium, living roof.
- Add Japanese Tea Garden / Botanical Garden if energy is high.
- Casual dinner nearby in Inner Sunset or NoPa.

Alternative:
- Aquarium of the Bay + sea lions at Pier 39 + waterfront walk.
- Exploratorium if you want interactive/creative science instead of a museum-heavy day.

## Wednesday, June 3 — Creative/artsy SF day

- Keep the existing calendar item: Walk with Seana at 5:30 PM.
- Earlier: SFMOMA or de Young / Legion of Honor.
- Creative add-on: Mission murals walk, Fort Mason, or a pottery/craft/paint workshop.
- Night: independent cinema at Roxie / Balboa / Alamo Drafthouse, or a small live show.

## Thursday, June 4 — Birthday centerpiece

Recommended centerpiece:
- Morning: Muir Woods or Marin Headlands / Rodeo Beach. Book Muir Woods parking/shuttle in advance if choosing Muir Woods.
- Midday: Sausalito lunch or scenic coffee stop.
- Afternoon: rest/reset, get dressed up.
- Dinner: skip Foreign Cinema because you've already been. Better birthday alternatives:
  - **Dalida** — Presidio date-night, warm, celebratory, pairs well with a Presidio / Crissy Field / Palace of Fine Arts moment.
  - **Greens** — waterfront/nature-adjacent, special without being overly formal; good if vegetarian-forward works.
  - **State Bird Provisions / The Progress** — playful, creative SF food energy if reservations work.
  - **Nopa** — classic SF, reliable celebratory neighborhood dinner.
  - **Flour + Water** — pasta-focused, intimate, Mission-side if the night includes Roxie / Alamo / Mission plans.
- After: tiny cake/candle + 26-photo/polaroid mini album + handwritten letter.

If staying in SF:
- Lands End + Sutro Baths + Legion of Honor, then birthday dinner.

## Friday, June 5 — Recovery + beach/sunset close

- Slow morning.
- Afternoon: Lands End / Sutro Baths / Ocean Beach OR Presidio Tunnel Tops / Crissy Field.
- Sunset: Baker Beach or Twin Peaks depending on fog.
- Dinner: Outer Richmond / Inner Sunset / Mission depending on film venue.
- Optional final film: Balboa, Roxie, Alamo Drafthouse, or Castro Theatre if programming lines up.

## Booking / prep checklist

- Reserve birthday dinner for Thu Jun 4. Top alternatives to Foreign Cinema: Dalida, Greens, State Bird Provisions / The Progress, Nopa, or Flour + Water.
- Book Muir Woods parking/shuttle if choosing Muir Woods.
- Check Cal Academy, Exploratorium, Roxie, Alamo Drafthouse, Balboa, Castro showtimes closer to the date.
- Buy flowers, cake/cupcakes, and a small creature/nature/film-themed gift.
- Make a "26 in SF" mini field guide / scavenger card.
- Pack layers for fog/wind.

## Gift ideas

- 26-photo/polaroid mini album from your relationship + first SF memories.
- Disposable camera for the week with a note: "document your first SF birthday."
- National parks / botanical / nature field guide.
- Handmade map of the week with tiny annotations.
- Film-themed touch: custom ticket stubs, Letterboxd-inspired card, or a private list of 26 movies to watch together.
